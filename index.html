About the summit

Geneva is built on international cooperation. This summit is for people here asking how to do it well.

EA Summit: Geneva 2026 brings together professionals who want to reason carefully about impact and leave with something concrete to act on. Effective altruism is a growing global movement dedicated to doing good more effectively — using evidence and careful reasoning to find the highest-leverage ways to help others.

Rather than a broad survey of causes, the day is built around real decisions: how people weigh trade-offs, choose where their work matters most, and change course when the evidence says they should. You'll hear from speakers who've built careers around that question, and leave with a concrete next step.

Why attend

Get sharper thinking about impact, concrete ways your existing skills could contribute to high-leverage work, and a first real connection to the EA community in Geneva.

What to expect

Talks and structured conversations on global health, biosecurity, AI, and animal welfare — plus 1-on-1s and small-group discussions centred on your own career, not a general Q&A.

Who it's for

Primarily Geneva-based professionals with a policy or international background, new to EA.

02

03

04

Program

A full day, built around decisions rather than a survey of causes.

Running 09:00–18:00 on Saturday, 24 October, in English. The detailed agenda will be published closer to the event — here's what to expect.

  • Real decisions, not surveys of causes

    Sessions built around how people actually weigh trade-offs and choose where their work will matter most — not a theoretical tour of cause areas.

  • Frameworks for judging impact

    Practical tools for weighing trade-offs, drawn from how practitioners apply them in their day-to-day work.

  • Career-change stories

    Peers who changed careers, or changed how they work, to do more good — what it cost them, what surprised them, what they'd do differently. Stories spanning global health, AI safety, humanitarian response, and animal welfare.

  • Structured 1-1s and small groups

    Conversations centred on your own career or work, not a general audience Q&A.

  • One concrete next step

    A specific opportunity, a connection, or something to try in your current role on Monday.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is there a cost to attend?

Yes, but we use an honor-system pricing model with three tiers (CHF 10–40) so you can choose what fits your situation. If cost would otherwise stop you from attending, email conferences@effectivealtruism.ch to request a free ticket.

I'm new to effective altruism — is this summit for me?

Yes. The summit is designed primarily for people with little or no prior exposure to EA, especially Geneva-based professionals with a policy or international background.

I'm already familiar with EA — can I still attend?

Yes. People already working in EA-aligned roles are welcome, particularly if you're interested in networking, mentoring attendees, or supporting community-building efforts in the Geneva region.

How does the application and payment process work?

You select a ticket and complete a short application. Our team reviews it and responds within 10 working days. Your card is only charged once your application is accepted.

Can I propose a session?

We're actively designing the program and accepting proposals via our session proposal form.

Who do I contact with other questions?

Email the organizing team at conferences@effectivealtruism.ch with any comments, suggestions, or questions.
